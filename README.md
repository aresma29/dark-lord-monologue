A 100-word villain monologue for the make.fun bounty. The Dark Lord has conquered seven kingdoms, commands legions and a doomsday device — and all he really wants is the small chair by the fire and a nap. The joke lands because the menace never drops: the kettle is empty, the candles burn low, the paperwork breeds in the dark, and even a tired villain keeps his appointments with the dark. Exactly one hundred words, counted, with the weariness doing the work that a threat normally would.

> "You stand before me, hero, sword raised, destiny rehearsed — and all I can think about is the chair. Not the throne; the small one, by the fire, where the cushions remember my shape. I conquered seven kingdoms before breakfast, or was it Tuesday? The prophecies blur. My dark legions march, my spies whisper, my doomsday device hums along — and still the kettle is empty, the candles are burning low, the paperwork breeds in the dark. Slay me if you must. Only please, be quick about it. I have a nap scheduled, and even tired villains keep appointments with the dark."

## Run it

`main.py` prints the monologue stored in `entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
